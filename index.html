A FEW THINGS TO REMEMBER

Make an ordinary moment feel extraordinary.

You don't need an expensive camera to take a beautiful picture. Start with good light, creative angles and your own perspective.

01

Chase the golden hour

Shoot shortly after sunrise or before sunset.

02

Experiment with angles

Try low angles, reflections and silhouettes.

03

Keep it natural

Walk, look away and capture candid moments.

04

Notice the details

Leaves, shadows and reflections make great frames.

SLOW DOWN AND SEE
